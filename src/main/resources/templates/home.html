Government Scheme Highlights

Key Benefits of Kisan Rin Portal

Subsidized Interest Rate

Crop loans at subsidized interest rates starting from 7% p.a. with additional 3% prompt repayment incentive.

Digital Land Verification

Seamless online 7/12 land record authentication by local Talathi officers eliminating manual paperwork delays.

Fast Bank Processing

Automated credit scoring system for rapid loan processing and direct bank disbursement into farmer accounts.
